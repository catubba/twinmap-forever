Privacy

What Twinmap does
Twinmap has no account, no ads and no tracking. It connects to the internet in four cases only: to look for a newer version, on GitHub; to fetch the prices of your auction house from prices.twinmap.app, about once an hour, asking for that house and sending nothing about you; when you press Send in the Feedback window; and, if you turn on Help improve Twinmap, to send anonymous data about the world of Forever.

Messages you send from the Feedback window

What is sent
The text you write. The details shown in the window: the versions of Twinmap and of its addon, whether the game folder and the optional addons were found, the state of the map and of the link with the game. Your email address, if you give one. The log, if you tick its box: Twinmap's own record of what happened to it, with the name of your Windows account removed.
What is never sent
The names of your character, realm or account, any part of your screen, any file of the game.
Where it goes
To our server at api.twinmap.app, which runs on Cloudflare. It passes the message on and keeps no copy. It uses your IP address only to refuse a burst of messages, and does not record it. The message is then kept as an issue in a private repository on GitHub that only the developer can read. Cloudflare and GitHub are companies of the United States: your message may be handled outside the European Union, under the safeguards those companies commit to for such transfers (the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).
Why
To read what you report, fix it, and answer you if you left an address. The legal ground is our legitimate interest in handling the feedback you chose to send (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR).
For how long
Until your message is dealt with, and never longer than 12 months. Then it is deleted.
Your rights
You can ask to see, correct or delete what you sent: write to dev@twinmap.app from the address you gave, or quote your message so that we can find it. A message sent without an address can be found only by its text. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.
Who is responsible
The developer of Twinmap, Catubba, Italy. Contact: dev@twinmap.app.

Help improve Twinmap

What it is
A choice in Twinmap, off until you turn it on: in Settings, or when Twinmap asks you once. While it is on, Twinmap sends what helps it find you better inside dungeons and fill in what the map lacks, for everyone. One switch covers everything listed here.
What is sent
  • Pictures of your minimap inside dungeons and raids: when Twinmap cannot tell where you are, at most one every 10 seconds, and once in each part of the dungeon when it can. With each picture: the dungeon, the scale of the minimap, the place Twinmap found and how sure it was. At most 100 pictures in a run, about 50 KB each, sent when you leave the dungeon.
  • Where bosses and rare creatures die, or where you target a rare, inside dungeons: the number of the encounter or of the creature, and your place found on the minimap. Sent when you leave the dungeon.
  • From the open world, through the Twinmap addon: the place where you enter a dungeon; what a trainer teaches, at what level and for what cost, with your standing with the trainer's faction; the creatures, the objects you loot and the quest steps that Questie has no place for, each with your place on the map; and of a creature Questie does not know, or knows somewhere else, what the game shows when you meet it or talk to it: its name, level and kind, the windows it opens, what it sells and the quests it gives and takes. Numbers only, except a creature's name as the game gives it: nothing you type can get into this record. Sent when the game saves its settings, at logout or after a reload.
  • Twinmap's own errors and how long it takes to read the minimap, at most every half hour. The folders of your computer and the names of your Windows account, of your computer and of your characters are removed first; a line that still holds a folder is not sent.
  • With all of these: the version of Twinmap and of its addon, the build and the language of the game, and a random code that ties together the data of one run or one session and is new for the next.
  • Before sending, Twinmap asks our server what it still needs today, giving only the dungeon and its own version. The answer is not recorded.
What can be in a minimap picture
What the game draws in the minimap: the map, the dots of your group, the icons that the game and your addons put on it. While the mouse is over the minimap, and for a few seconds before and after, Twinmap takes no picture, so the names the game shows there are never sent. A window of another program over the game stops the pictures too.
What is never sent
Your name, characters, guild, account, group, chat, any other part of your screen, any file of the game, your IP address, a code that lasts beyond one run or session, or a trace of where you go in the open world.
Where it goes
To our server at api.twinmap.app, which runs on Cloudflare, and into storage of Cloudflare kept in the European Union. The server uses your IP address only to refuse a burst of sends, and does not record it. Cloudflare is a company of the United States: the data may pass through its servers outside the European Union, under the safeguards it commits to for such transfers (the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).
Why
To find where Twinmap puts the arrow in the wrong place and why, to correct the places of bosses, doors and creatures, and to show what trainers of other classes teach. What we learn goes into Twinmap for everyone (for example the spells and costs of a trainer, or the place of a boss); the data as it arrives is not published.
Legal ground
Your consent, because the data leaves your computer (Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive; Article 122 of the Italian privacy code). Nothing that is kept identifies you, so the data kept is anonymous and not personal data under the GDPR.
For how long
12 months, then the storage deletes it by itself.
Turning it off
Turn it off in Settings at any time: nothing more is sent, and what Twinmap was keeping to send is thrown away. The addon stops recording as soon as it hears it, and throws away what it had. What was already sent cannot be deleted on request: nothing in it says who sent it, so we cannot find yours.
Who is responsible
The developer of Twinmap, Catubba, Italy. Contact: dev@twinmap.app.
